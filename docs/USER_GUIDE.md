# Bidder Guide

## 1. Find a property
Open the website and use the search bar and filters: property type, location, price range, bidding status or reference number.
Each property page shows photos, details, the minimum bid, schedule, bidding rules, documents, terms and contact information.

## 2. Register
Click **Register to Bid** and provide:
- your full name (as shown on your ID), company name (if bidding for a company), complete address, mobile number and email;
- a password (at least 10 characters with upper- and lower-case letters and a number);
- your government ID type and number, and an upload of the ID (PDF, JPG or PNG).

Read and accept the Privacy Notice, the Data Privacy Consent and the Terms and Conditions, then complete the security check.
Each person or company may have **only one account**. Duplicate registrations are detected.

## 3. Verify your account
Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email, or click the link in the email. If SMS verification is enabled, also enter the code sent to your mobile.
Upload any other required documents under **My Documents**. Cityland reviews your registration and notifies you when it is approved.

## 4. Review the terms and rules
Each property shows its **bidding rules**: whether you can revise your bid, the minimum increment, whether you can withdraw, whether location is required, and whether automatic extension applies.
Read them before bidding.

## 5. Submit your bid
On the property page, the **Submit Bid** button stays disabled until every item in the requirements checklist has a ✓.
1. Enter your bid amount.
2. Choose **Share my location** or **Don't share**. Location is never collected without your action. If you decline, your bid records "Location permission not granted." Some events require location.
3. Tick the box to accept the Terms and Conditions.
4. Click **Submit Bid** and confirm.

## 6. Bid confirmation
You immediately get a **bid reference number** (for example `BID-20260927-7PXFY2`), an acknowledgment page you can **print or save as PDF**, and a confirmation email.

## 7. Monitor your status
**My Dashboard** shows the properties you joined, your bid references, amounts, dates and times, bidding status, verification status, documents, notifications and results.
Depending on the property's rules, you may also see your ranking, the highest bid amount or the number of bidders. Other bidders' identities are never shown.

## 8. Evaluation and award
All times use the Cityland server clock, and bidding closes exactly at the official closing time unless automatic extension applies.
After closing, Cityland evaluates the bidders. **The highest bid does not automatically win.**
You will receive an email saying whether you are the winning bidder, a backup bidder or not selected.

## Frequently asked questions
- **Can I change my bid?** Only if the property allows revised bids. Your earlier bids are kept on record.
- **Why is my bid rejected?** Check the minimum bid and increment, and confirm bidding is still open.
- **I forgot my password.** Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
- **Privacy concerns?** Contact the Data Protection Officer listed on the Privacy page.
